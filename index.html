Frequently asked questions

Do I need API keys or an extra subscription?
No. AI Capsule works with the AI chats you already use in your browser — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and more — through your existing accounts. There are no API keys to manage and no extra AI costs on top of what you already pay (or don't).
Which AI chats does it support?
Common Prompt and Automation work with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Qwen, Microsoft Copilot, Mistral's Le Chat and DeepSeek out of the box — plus 60+ more AI tools you can open in any workspace via the built-in AI Store.
Is my data safe?
Your workspaces, prompts and archived pages are stored locally on your device. AI Capsule doesn't run its own servers or collect your conversations — you're simply using the official web apps of the AI tools you already trust, inside a more organized browser.
Does it work offline?
Archived pages open fully offline. AI chats themselves still need an internet connection, same as using them in any regular browser.
